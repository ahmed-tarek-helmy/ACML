About EBSCO

   

EBSCO Information Services is a worldwide leader in providing information access and management solutions through print and electronic journal subscription services, research database development and production, online access to more than 100 databases and thousands of e-journals, and e-commerce book procurement. EBSCO has served the library and business communities for more than 60 years.
EBSCO Information Services' parent company, EBSCO Industries Inc., is a diversified, financially stable company with manufacturing and sales offices around the world. EBSCO's International Headquarters in Birmingham, Ala., U.S.A. provides support for all EBSCO operations.
